ArcaneBlacksmith25 - Reduces the Smithing requirement of the Arcane Blacksmith perk to 25. (also removes the Steel Smithing prerequisite to match Perks Unbound.)

BetterOrcishTwoHanded - Increases the damage of Orcish Greatswords, Battleaxes, and Warhammers to match their Elven/Nordic counterparts.

CraftableNightingaleArmor - Adds recipes for stronger, unenchanted versions of the Nightingale equipment. The armor is equivalent to the Dragonscale set, while the weapons and arrows are equivalent to their Dragonbone counterparts. All can be crafted from Ebony and Void Salts with the Daedric Smithing perk.

FasterCraftingXP - Increases skill gain for tempering and crafting.

FortifyUnarmedEnchantTweaks - Makes Insightful Enchanter perk affect Fortify Unarmed enchantment, and allows the latter to be applied to boots and necklaces.

FortifyUnarmedEnchantDoubleBasePower - Doubles the base power of the Fortify Unarmed enchantment. Recommended to enable in the late game to keep unarmed relatively competitive.

Morokei - Mage Armor compatible Morokei.

VRCCFistofRandagultFix - VR compatibility fix for the Fists of Randagulf from CC/Anniversary.
